Fix master-topology receiver-side session-generation drift that silently dropped every forwarded publish from an authenticated peer. ``_verify_session`` now compares the frame's ``session_generation`` against the server-side ``_INBOUND[origin]`` record (established at handshake and kept current by ``_maybe_lazy_register_inbound``), not the unrelated outbound ``_LINKS[origin]`` counter. Mesh-wide ``state.apply`` now returns real state-result dicts from every minion instead of ``{"failed": True}`` for forwarded hops.
