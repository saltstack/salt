Give each ``AsyncAuth.authenticate()`` caller its own Future over a shared sign-in task, replacing the single ``_authenticate_future`` attribute that was mutable instance state shared by every caller. The previous design conflated "handle to await the sign-in result" with "flag that a sign-in is in progress"; a stale ``_authenticate`` coroutine reaching its tail could hit a future already resolved by a sibling and raise ``asyncio.exceptions.InvalidStateError``, killing the task and wedging the minion in a non-authenticated state until process restart. The new design also propagates unexpected exceptions (``OSError`` and others that previously escaped ``_authenticate`` as unhandled task exceptions) to every waiter instead of hanging them indefinitely, and cancels the sign-in task when the last waiter leaves so it does not hold its request channel open.
