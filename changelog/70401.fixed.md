Fix ``salt-key -a/-r`` acceptance not replicating to cluster peers on an isolated-filesystem master cluster.  ``Key.change_state`` fired the ``salt/key`` event with the long-form act (``"accepted"``/``"rejected"``/``"pending"``), but ``EventMonitor._apply_peer_key_change`` matches on the short form (``"accept"``/``"reject"``/``"pend"``), so peers silently dropped the event.  Auto-accept and ``salt-key -d`` were unaffected because those paths already fired the short form directly.
