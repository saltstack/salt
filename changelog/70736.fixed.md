Demote six residual shutdown-time log sites from ERROR to DEBUG
(follow-up to PR #70733): ``RaftDispatcher._reply`` peer-unreachable;
``tcp.RequestServer.close`` EBADF; ``zeromq.RequestServer.close``
IOLoop-already-closed; ``ZeroMQSocketMonitor.consume`` ENOTSOCK/ETERM/EINTR;
redundant "Master is unavailable (Connection Cancelled)" ERROR on the
receive-poll CancelledError path; and ``tcp.MessageClient._stream_return``
parse-exception while ``_closing``/``_closed`` is already set. Unexpected
exceptions on each of those paths continue to log at ERROR.
