Fix spurious ``unclosed SyncWrapper`` / ``unclosed publisher client`` warnings emitted from the minion after a clean shutdown. ``PublishServer.close`` silently swallowed exceptions from ``pub_sock.close()`` (typically ``RuntimeError: Event loop is closed`` on daemon-restart retry) and cleared the reference regardless, orphaning the wrapper — its finalizer then emitted the warnings, misleading operators into hunting a leak that had already been released. ``close`` now logs the swallowed exception with ``exc_info=True`` and falls back to closing the wrapped ``_TCPPubServerPublisher`` directly.
