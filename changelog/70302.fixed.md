Fixed `PublishServer.close` orphaning its `pub_sock` SyncWrapper when the inner `SyncWrapper.close` raised (e.g. `RuntimeError: Event loop is closed` during minion daemon-restart retry cycles): the broad-except now logs with `exc_info=True` and falls back to closing the wrapped `_TCPPubServerPublisher` directly so its socket FD and `msgpack.Unpacker` buffer are always released. Follow-up to #70175.
