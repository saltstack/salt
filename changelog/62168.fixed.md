Avoid `salt-master` startup stalls on macOS caused by reverse-DNS on link-local, site-local, or RFC1918 interface addresses. `network.fqdns` now skips addresses that cannot be answered by public DNS and time-bounds the remaining lookups; `grains.core.ip_fqdn` bounds forward-lookup latency so a stuck mDNS resolver can't stall grain refresh. `salt.config.mminion_config` reuses a caller-provided grains dict instead of recomputing on every `MasterMinion` instantiation, collapsing dozens of redundant refreshes during startup to one.
