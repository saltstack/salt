Avoid `salt-master` startup stalls on macOS caused by reverse-DNS on link-local, site-local, or RFC1918 interface addresses. `network.fqdns` now skips addresses that cannot be answered by public DNS, time-bounds and caches the remaining lookups, and `grains.core.ip_fqdn` bounds forward-lookup latency so a stuck mDNS resolver can't stall grain refresh.
