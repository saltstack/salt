Suppress async cleanup races on salt-master/minion SIGTERM: no more
``Task was destroyed but it is pending!`` for ``PubServer._drain_loop``
or ``ZeroMQSocketMonitor.consume``, and the Raft leader no longer
floods ERROR tracebacks for every peer that is going away during
``docker compose down`` (now DEBUG-level, cached dead-peer client is
invalidated so the next heartbeat reconnects).
