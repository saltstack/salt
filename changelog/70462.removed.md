Removed deprecated transport class shims that reached their Potassium (3009.0) deadline:

- ``salt.transport.tcp.TCPPubClient`` — use ``salt.transport.tcp.PublishClient``.
- ``salt.transport.tcp.TCPReqServer`` — use ``salt.transport.tcp.RequestServer``.
- ``salt.transport.tcp.TCPPublishServer`` — use ``salt.transport.tcp.PublishServer``.
- ``salt.transport.tcp.TCPReqClient`` — use ``salt.transport.tcp.RequestClient``.

The ``MessageClient`` (salt.transport.tcp) and ``AsyncReqMessageClient`` (salt.transport.zeromq) classes retain their current implementations; the stale deprecation warning on their ``__init__`` has been dropped since they remain the canonical low-level transport primitives.
