Fix `topology link_manager._handle_master_auth` incrementing `session_generation` on every call, which caused frames from the peer master's secondary Raft processes to fail `_verify_session` after the first auth landed. When the peer master is itself a 3008.x master cluster, each of its master processes authenticates independently against the same `cluster_id` identity, so bumping on every auth left earlier processes pinned to a stale gen. The handler now keeps the generation stable across re-auths and returns `session_generation` in the reply so the sender's `PeerMasterLink._rotate_session` can pin its counter to match.
