Fixed the TCP transport clients retrying failed master connections at a fixed interval forever. The publish and request clients now honor the ``recon_default``, ``recon_max`` and ``recon_randomize`` minion options the same way the ZeroMQ transport does: the delay doubles after each failure in a row up to ``recon_max``, the initial delay is randomized to keep minions from retrying in step, and the sequence starts over after a connection succeeds. ``tcp_reconnect_backoff`` remains the TCP-specific override of ``recon_default`` when configured to a non-default value, and a publish connection the master closes right after the TLS handshake is retried after the backoff instead of immediately.
