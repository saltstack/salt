Fix Raft dispatcher silently dropping peer replies when `opts["cluster_peers"]` drifts out of order with `self.pushers`. `build_peer_pushers` now keys the dispatcher's pusher table by each pusher's own `pull_host` (matching the robust `_start_raft_as_learner` path) so a duplicate `cluster_peers.append` on a join/discover from a statically-configured peer, or a `host:port` entry in config, no longer leaves a legitimate peer unkeyed and spamming `RaftDispatcher: no pusher for <peer>, dropping <tag> reply`. The matching append sites in `MasterPubServerChannel` also dedup before extending `self.cluster_peers`.
