Fixed two defects that kept a master cluster off IPv6-only hosts. The cluster pool puller was handed `opts["interface"]` after `salt.cli.daemons` had rewritten it into bracketed form for URIs, and `socket.bind()` rejects `[::]`, so the peer port never opened; the brackets are now stripped at the bind. Separately, the TCP publisher guessed a peer's address family from whether its name contained a colon, so a `cluster_peers` entry given as a hostname that only resolves to AAAA got an `AF_INET` socket and failed with "No address associated with hostname" surfaced as a `StreamClosedError`; resolution now decides the family.
