Windows master-cluster peers no longer hang in `Master.start()` between
"Creating master publisher process" and `salt.engines.start_engines`.  The
primary `MasterPubServerChannel.transport` on Windows clustered now binds
the event-bus ports (`tcp_master_pub_port` / `tcp_master_pull_port`) on
the `_ipc_loopback` literal instead of colliding with the regular
`PubServerChannel` on `publish_port`.
