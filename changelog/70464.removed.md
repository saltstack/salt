Removed deprecated kwargs and functions that reached their Potassium (3009.0) deadline:

- ``salt.modules.win_useradd.getUserSid`` — use ``get_user_sid``.
- ``salt.modules.macdefaults.write``'s ``type`` kwarg — use ``vtype``.
- ``salt.modules.zypperpkg.mod_repo``'s ``humanname`` kwarg — use ``name``.
- ``salt.states.pkgrepo.managed``'s ``allow_insecure_key`` now defaults to ``False`` (previously defaulted to ``True`` with a deprecation warning).
- ``salt.utils.event.SaltEvent.fire_event_async``'s ``cb`` kwarg (no replacement; use ``await`` on the coroutine).
