Fixed the minion leaking its `MinionManager` event resources when it lost its master connection: `cli.daemons.Minion.start` now destroys the `MinionManager` whenever `_real_start` raises `SaltClientError`, both before a daemonized multi-master failover retry and before returning, instead of leaving `event_publisher` and `event` to be reclaimed by `__del__` at garbage-collection time.
