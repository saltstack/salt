Fixed minion engines failing with ``KeyError: 'master_uri'`` when calling execution modules that need the master transport, such as ``pillar.data``. Engines now resolve ``master_uri`` themselves once the minion has created its key pair, so an engine never generates keys of its own on a first start. Engines of failover and distributed minions, whose ``master`` is a list, are still started without ``master_uri``.
