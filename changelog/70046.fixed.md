Fixed ``salt.utils.x509.load_pubkey`` silently ignoring the ``get_encoding`` argument. When ``get_encoding=True``, the function now returns a ``(public_key, encoding, None)`` tuple (with ``encoding`` being ``None``, ``"pem"`` or ``"der"``), matching the contract of its sibling ``load_privkey``. The default behavior (returning the loaded public key object) is unchanged, and no existing caller passes ``get_encoding=True``.
