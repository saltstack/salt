Bound the master-side ws publish paths with an optional timeout. `salt.transport.ws.PublishServer.publish` now accepts `timeout=` and raises `TimeoutError` instead of waiting forever if `StreamWriter.drain` wedges; `PublishServer.publish_payload` applies the same bound per-client and, on timeout, drops the stalled subscriber from `self.clients` (same remediation as `ConnectionResetError`) and continues the fan-out so one slow minion cannot stop publishes to every other minion. Mirrors the asyncio.wait + late-observe pattern from #70461 (TCP publisher) into the ws wire-transport seams. Defense-in-depth for `transport: ws` masters -- no known production incident, closes a correctness gap matching #70461.
