Fixed the minion freezing in ``zmq_ctx_term()`` after a cancelled sign-in. The shutdown sequence now cancels *and awaits* every outstanding asyncio task before the event loop is closed, so each task's ``async with AsyncReqChannel`` ``__aexit__`` runs and closes its REQ socket inline. Without this, the ``MinionManager``/``Minion``/``SyndicManager`` ``tune_in()`` finally would close the loop with sign-in tasks still in the ``cancelling`` state; the orphan socket then blocked ``zmq_ctx_term()`` on the next ``gc.collect()`` and froze the process until a restart.
