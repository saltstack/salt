Fixed the minion crashing with ``RuntimeError: Event loop is closed`` when running in classic daemon mode (``-d``) and retrying after a failed master reconnect. ``cli.daemons.Minion.start()``'s retry loop now constructs a fresh ``MinionManager`` instead of re-entering ``tune_in()`` on the stale, closed-loop instance, and several client/event teardown paths that could themselves raise on an already-closed loop are now guarded.
