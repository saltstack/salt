The minion no longer leaves zmq sockets open when it stops or restarts in-process: ``MinionManager.tune_in()`` now cancels the tasks still on its event loop and lets them finish before the loop is closed. A task such as a sign-in that waited for a master that was down was left suspended with its socket open, and a later garbage collection blocked in ``zmq_ctx_term()``, freezing the minion.
