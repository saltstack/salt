Bounded the DNS lookups made while collecting grains (`hostname`'s call to `salt.utils.network.get_fqhostname()`, the `fqdn_ip4`/`fqdn_ip6` grains, and the `fqdns` grain's reverse lookups) with a new `grains_dns_lookup_timeout` minion option (default 5s), so `salt-call --local` and other grains refreshes no longer hang for as long as the OS resolver takes when the minion's hostname has no usable DNS/hosts entry. Also removed the `__role == "master"` gate on the related timeout warning so `--local`/minion runs get a diagnostic instead of silence.
