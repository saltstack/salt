``network.arp``, ``network.ip_neighs`` and ``network.ip_neighs6`` no longer emit bogus entries for unresolved neighbours: ``arp -an`` rows whose MAC column is the ``<incomplete>``/``(incomplete)`` placeholder, and ``ip neigh`` rows that carry a flag such as ``router`` but no ``lladdr`` (which previously leaked the state word, e.g. ``FAILED``, as the MAC key).
