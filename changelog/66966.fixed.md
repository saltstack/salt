Fixed requests over the TCP transport never timing out while the master is down or unreachable, which kept a minion from failing over to its next master. The timeout now also runs while the client is connecting. As with zeromq, a request fails once its timeout and tries are used up instead of waiting for the master to come back, so job returns sent during a master outage are no longer delivered once the master is back. Concurrent requests on a new connection, such as a master's requests to its worker pools, now share one connection attempt instead of opening one each, which could fail them with `Already reading`.
