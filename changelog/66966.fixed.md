Fixed requests over the TCP transport never timing out while the master is down or unreachable, which kept a minion from failing over to its next master. The timeout now also runs while the client is connecting. Requests made while the master is unreachable now fail once their timeout and tries are used up, as they do on zeromq, instead of waiting for the master to come back, so job returns sent during a master outage are no longer delivered once the master is back. Concurrent requests on a new connection, such as a master's requests to its worker pools, now share one connection attempt instead of opening one each, which could fail them with `Already reading`.
