Quiet the minion bootstrap race against a clustered master. When a clustered
master defers requests with the ``cluster_retry`` sentinel while its Raft node
catches up, the pillar fetch now logs at ``DEBUG`` (not ``ERROR``) and the
multi-master connect loop logs a single ``INFO`` retry notice (not an ``ERROR``
with traceback). The connect loop also resets ``_running`` after ``destroy()``
so the retry actually binds the master instead of tripping the misleading
"scheduled to stop" branch.
