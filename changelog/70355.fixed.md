The ``RemotePillar`` built during minion start-up (``SMinion.gen_modules``, ``SProxyMinion.gen_modules``) and by ``pillar.items``, ``pillar.ext``, ``cp`` and ``cmdmod`` pillar gathering was never destroyed, so its request channel was only closed at interpreter shutdown.
